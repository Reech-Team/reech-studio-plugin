# Make · The cut of a talking recording

Part of the `make` skill: everything there (the order of work, the look, the checks, delivery) still holds.

**The cut (a recording of a person talking): the first thing that makes it look edited.** A raw take has stumbles, retakes and detours; leaving them in is the first thing a viewer notices, before any animation. The cut is the person's: many creators edit, and know exactly what they want out. Your job is to make it easy and exact, whichever way they work. Ask once: *"Do you want to cut it yourself in your app, decide it with me sentence by sentence, or should I propose a cut for you to correct?"* Every way ends in the same file, `<recording>.cut.json`, which the config plays: `import cut from "../../../../../../../public/brands/<slug>/<folder>/<name>.cut.json";` (same depth as the words import) and `keep: cut.keep`.
- **They cut it themselves** (CapCut, Edits, Premiere, their phone): they upload their exported edit next to the raw recording (upload page, same folder), `media_transcribe` both, then `media_cut` with `path` = the raw recording and `edit` = their export. Their cut is found word by word in the original and plays on the original footage at full quality, with the pauses they shortened shortened, and the lines they moved moved; the design then goes on top of their decisions. Checked on a creator's real export against her 10-minute recording: 99% of her words found, her 3:10 rebuilt to within a second, her order kept. The sheet that comes back marks what their edit kept; read it to learn their editing taste (point 4).
- **They decide it with you:** `media_cut` on the raw recording returns the **sheet**: every sentence numbered (`s1`, `s2`…), marked ✓ kept, ~ trimmed (dropped words in [brackets]: a false start, a filler) or ✗ dropped with the reason, and lines said again far apart. Show it to them (it reads like a script) and change it as they say, by sentence: `media_cut` with `order: ["s1", "s30", "s9", "s11", …]` (played in that order, so "move the answer up" is moving its id), `"s12!"` to keep a take exactly as said (stumble included, when they want it), `"s12:3-9"` for only part of a sentence. Each call rewrites the cut and returns the new sheet and length; then a proxy of the bare cut with sound (`video_proxy`, no phrases yet) so they hear the flow.
- **You propose, they correct:** steps 1–3 below, then the same sheet loop.
1. **Clean (mechanical, by code):** `media_cut` without `order` is the cleanup's proposal: false starts out (a cut-off word like `usted--` and the attempt it abandoned, keeping the take said right after), a sentence said again within 15 s (the last take stays), hesitation sounds (`eh`, `eeeh`, `mmm`) and pauses over 0.35 s. Lines said again far apart (often the hook recorded twice) are flagged: choose one take. Checked on a creator's own edit of a 10-minute recording: everything it dropped, she had dropped too, and it caught all 9 false starts. (In code, the same is `cutSheet(words)` / `roughCut(words)` from `src/components/rough-cut.ts`.)
2. **Edit (judgment, yours, then the person's):** read the cleaned sheet as a script and write the cut as an `order` of sentence ids, each dropped one with its reason. What a good edit of a talking video does (the same creator took 635 s to 190 s): the hook question once, then **the answer right away** (move it up from wherever it was said: "I decided to stay in Peru. Why?"), then the reasons, each said once, in its most concrete take (examples and details stay: the wooden toys, the cold-water fountains); out go the preamble ("this video is improvised"), disclaimers ("don't judge me", "it's just my perspective"), the elaboration after a point is already made, lists of alternatives that go nowhere, and connectors that only fill ("entonces", "en fin", "o sea" at a sentence start); the invitation to comment moves to the end; it ends on a line with a punch, not on a fade. The video's target length (the plan) decides how far to go. Then `media_cut` with that `order`.
3. **Show the cut before anything else is built:** the kept text in order, the moves ("answer moved from 1:55 to the start"), the dropped parts grouped by reason, the length. That's the person's checkpoint for the voice (Ángel's order: voice first, then highlights, then animation). A proxy of the bare cut with sound (`video_proxy`, no phrases yet) when they want to hear the flow. Never redo a cut they made: suggest ("s8 is a preamble; drop it?"), they decide.
4. **When the person has already edited a recording themselves** (their own version of this video, or an earlier one), `media_cut` with their `edit` and compare its sheet with the cleanup's proposal on the same recording: what they dropped beyond it and what they moved is their editing taste. Write it into section 0 of the brand's `style/catalog.md` ("answer first", "no disclaimers", "ends on a punchline"), so the next cut starts from it, and `craft_record` each one brand-neutral (`area: "cut"`, `source.kind: "manual"` with the brand and video), so the organization's other brands learn it too.
