# Make · Editing rounds

Part of the `make` skill: everything there (the order of work, the look, the checks, delivery) still holds.

## 0b. Editing rounds: numbered, recorded, never lost

The back-and-forth after the first preview is where the video becomes theirs. Make it a clear loop. **Their dashboard** shows it all visually: `me` → `dashboardVideo` (`…/app#/v/<brand>/<campaign>/<video>`); give them that link with the first preview. There they watch previews (every finished preview is kept as a version and they can compare two side by side, so record each round's `proxy` job id in `rounds.json`), ask for changes (written into `rounds.json` with `via: "dashboard"`; a note made while the preview was paused carries `time`, the second on that round's preview, and may carry `box`, the area they drew on the frame (`{ x, y, w, h }` as fractions of the picture, from its top-left): look at that exact moment with `video_stills` `times: [time]`, find what sits inside the box, and change that, not something else nearby), approve a round, reorder or drop sentences of the cut (a new `media_cut`, also noted in `rounds.json`), and tune a saved style's colors and sizes (its `look.json`, with a reason line saying they set it). They may have Claude in Chrome open beside it.
0. **Start every edit round with `video_state`** (one small call): the words actually spoken with their indices (`"0:Hola 1:a 2:todos…"`; `times: true` adds each word's second for cuts and trims), the compositions and aspects the video has, the live edits and the ids `undo` can take, the ops and fields `video_edit` can change (and which are locked), and the voice in use. Take words (a word op's `i`, what an `at: { re }` must match), edit ids and aspects from it, never from memory or an earlier round: a word that isn't in the voice-over, `word 999`, `undo: ["e6"]` when only e1–e3 exist, or a `1:1` proxy on a 9:16-only video each cost a dead round. Voices come from `voices_list`, never a remembered id.
   **Then read what they did by hand** (the round with `stage: "script"` is the script's, from `plan`; preview rounds are numbered from 1) (their dashboard's Home also lists what waits on you, so leave nothing open): `video_get` → `rounds` (items with `via: "dashboard"` and `status: "open"` are theirs: apply them, then set `status: "done"` and fill `change` with what you did) and, if they edited the cut, re-read the `.cut.json` (the config already plays its `keep`). A round they approved in the dashboard is approved. Never undo a manual change without asking.
1. Each preview you give them is a **round**: `video_proxy` with sound (`from`/`to` for a section when only that changed), its link, and one line on what changed since the last round.
2. Their feedback goes into `rounds.json` in the video folder (`file_put`; `video_get` returns it as `rounds`): a JSON **array of rounds**, each `{ round, proxy: "<job id>", date, feedback: [{ said, change, scope: "this video" | "style", by, via: "chat" | "dashboard", status: "open" | "done" }], approved: false }`, one item per thing they said, in their words and as what you'll do. *"The text is too small at the start"* → `{ said, change: "hook title 120 → 160 px", scope: "style" }`. Ask only when an item is ambiguous.
3. Apply all of a round's items at once, then the next preview; never re-litigate what an earlier round settled.
   **Every tweak after the first version goes through `video_edit`, not a config rewrite.** One call takes all of a round's changes (`ops`, up to 30), checks the video still builds, saves them to the video's `edits.json` (applied over `config.ts` on every preview and render) and returns a short preview of the seconds they touched (`links.video`): that preview is the round's (record its job id). Point at things the way the video's `timeline.json` lists them (`file_get`; every preview rewrites it): the cut, every spoken word with its index `i` and its seconds, the phrases, labels, equations, sound cues, b-roll and texts with their index in `config.ts`. The ops:
   - a word as it reads in captions and phrases: `{ op: "word", t: 12.4, text: "Perú" }` (or `i`);
   - take a stretch out: `{ op: "cut", from: 20.1, to: 22.8 }` (seconds on the video; it snaps to whole words);
   - a cut shorter or longer: `{ op: "trim", t: 31.2, side: "end", by: -0.3 }` (the cut playing at `t`; negative = shorter at its end, later at its start);
   - any value: `{ op: "set", path: "phrases.3.text", value: "…" }`, `cues.2.file` (swap a sound), `cues.2.volume`, `captions.scale`, `music.volume`, `hook.text`, `labels.0.side`, `zoom`; `remove: true` takes an item out;
   - add to a list: `{ op: "add", path: "cues", value: { at: { re: "^agua" }, file, lufs } }`.
   A change that doesn't fit (a phrase on a word the cut just removed) is refused with the reason and nothing is saved: fix the op. `undo: ["e4"]` takes an edit back (the ids are in `video_state` → `edits.undo`, and in each result). Edit `config.ts` itself only to build something new (a new layout, a new style); after that, keep tweaking with `video_edit`.
   **Requests from their dashboard** (they pause the preview and tap a word, the text on screen, the sound or the cut) come as feedback items with an `id`, `said` in their words, `time`, and `ask`: `kind` is the op (`word`, `cut`, `trim`, `set`) and `target` + `value` (or `remove: true`) its fields, so it is exact: do it as asked (`{ kind: "word", target: { i: 57 }, value: "Perú" }` → `{ op: "word", i: 57, text: "Perú" }`; `{ kind: "set", target: { path: "cues.1" }, remove: true }` → `{ op: "set", path: "cues.1", remove: true }`). `kind: "idea"` (another sound, highlight something here, bigger captions, with `want` in their words) is yours to decide: pick the sound (`media_sfx`), write the phrase, choose the size, in the brand's style. Apply all of them in one `video_edit` (`request`: the first item's id), then set each item's `status: "done"` and `change` to what you did. They paste *"Aplica los cambios que pedí en el dashboard…"* into the chat when they're ready: that's the signal.
4. "Go back to round N" is the config as it was then: keep each round's config (`video_get` gives the history through `rounds`; save a copy at `rounds/<n>.config.txt` with `file_put` before changing it: text, never `.ts`, so the build doesn't pick it up).
5. Mark `scope: "style"` for anything that will hold for their next videos (sizes, colors, pace, what they never want, how they cut) and `"this video"` for what's about this content. The style items become rules when the style is saved.
6. **Every `scope: "style"` item also goes to the organization's craft store**, in the same round (from the chat or the dashboard): `craft_record` with the lesson **brand-neutral**, as the rule itself and where it holds (*"subtítulos muy rápidos al inicio"* → `{ lesson: "Captions enter on the spoken word, ≤ 6 frames, never ahead of the voice", area: "captions", when: { case: "talking-head" }, source: { kind: "round", brand, campaign, video, round, feedback: "<item id>", said: "<their words>" } }`). Never a brand name, product, color hex or client in the lesson: those stay in the brand's ficha. When `craft_list` already has the same lesson, pass `same: "<id>"` so it counts as more evidence. Then write the returned id on the item (`craft: "<id>"`) so it isn't recorded twice. The store is shared by all of this organization's brands (a creator's lessons follow her to her next client), never by other organizations. If the answer lists `ready` clusters, mention them in one line at the end of the round ("this came up in 3 videos: shall I make it a default?") and leave the promotion to Learn or to their yes (`craft_propose`, then `craft_promote`).
