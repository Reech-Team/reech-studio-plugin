Take $ARGUMENTS from a cut to a delivered video, following section 5 and 6 of the `make` skill. Final renders count against the organization's monthly limit: render once the script is approved and the stills pass your look (`make` section 4). Don't ask the person to approve the cut first.

1. `video_render` (`lufs: -14` for social; pass `aspect` when the config has several). `job_wait` until done; if it fails, read the error, fix the config (`video_save`), check with `video_stills`, and render again.
2. `video_qa` on that render, then `video_audit` right away (the audit waits for that QA on the server); wait for both, look at the QA sheet and the transcript result. Fix every block and every warn the brand hasn't overridden; re-render when the fix changes the picture or the sound.
3. Creative review: watch the proxy and stills at every beat, judge the render as a viewer against the concept, and write `review.md` (`video_save` with `review` and `etags.review`: `render: <the render's file name, e.g. a-antiacne.mp4>`, `verdict: pass|fix`, the seven answers). A `fix` verdict means fix and start over at step 1.
4. `video_deliver`. Then write the post copy into `deliver/post.md` (`file_get` the template, `file_put` the copy: a hook line that isn't the video's first line, the CTA, 3–5 hashtags, no TODOs left).
5. Give the person the links (`media_links` on the MP4, cover and SRT under `media/brands/<slug>/deliverables/<campaign>/` and the video's `deliver/` folder) and a 2–3 line summary. For a brand's first video, ask them to watch it once.
